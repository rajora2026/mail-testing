Legal & Privacy

Privacy & Usage Policy

How the BetterGrowth Solutions internal mail system collects, uses and protects information, and what is expected of everyone who signs in.

Effective: 4 October 2026Applies to all 5 staff accounts@bettergrowthsolutions.com
On this page Scope Information we collect How we use it Monitoring & admin access Storage & browser data Security Acceptable use Retention & deletion Your choices Changes Contact

01Scope

This policy covers the private web mail system operated by BetterGrowth Solutions for its team. It applies to anyone given an account, including the administrator. The system is for business communication only and is not a public service.

Accounts are created by the administrator. Signing in means you have read and accepted this policy.

02Information we collect

DataWhy
Account details: work email address, display nameTo identify you and show who emails are from
Emails you send and receive: recipients, CC/BCC, subject, body, attachments, status, timeTo deliver mail and keep your sent and inbox history
Login and logout times, session ID, device/browser typeSecurity, audit and spotting unusual sign-ins
Activity log entries (for example sending, viewing or deleting an email)Accountability and troubleshooting
Profile photo and device list (saved only in your browser)Personalisation; not sent to our server

We do not collect payment details, and we do not sell or share your data for advertising.

03How we use it

  • Sign you in and keep your session active.
  • Send, receive, store and display your email.
  • Protect the system against misuse, unauthorised access and abuse.
  • Investigate incidents and meet legal or contractual obligations.

04Monitoring & admin access

This is a company system. The administrator (admin@bettergrowthsolutions.com) can:

  • See all emails sent and received through the system, across every account.
  • View the activity log and sign-in sessions for each user.
  • Send mail on behalf of the company addresses (for example hr@, accounting@).
Do not use this mail system for personal or private correspondence. You should not expect privacy from the administrator for anything sent or received here.

05Storage & browser data

Mail and logs are stored with our backend provider (Supabase). Your browser keeps a small amount of data so you stay signed in: a session token, your user details, a session ID and the login time. Your profile photo and device list stay only in your browser.

Signing out records a logout event and removes the sign-in data from that browser. On shared computers always sign out and close the tab.

06Security

  • Connections use HTTPS.
  • Each sign-in needs your password and a verification code.
  • Received email content is displayed in an isolated frame so scripts inside it cannot run.
  • Never share your password. Report any suspicious activity straight away.

No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to reduce risk.

07Acceptable use

You agree not to:

  • Send spam, scams, harassing, illegal or misleading messages.
  • Impersonate another person or send from an address you are not authorised to use.
  • Attach malware or try to bypass the system's security.
  • Share customer or company confidential information with people who should not have it.
  • Access another user's account or data.

Breaking these rules may lead to account suspension and further action.

08Retention & deletion

Emails and logs are kept while your account is active and as long as the business needs them. Deleting an email removes it from your view and the system. When someone leaves, the administrator may disable the account and keep records as needed for business or legal reasons.

09Your choices

You can ask the administrator to show, correct or delete personal details we hold about you, subject to business and legal record-keeping needs. You can clear your browser data at any time to remove the locally stored photo and device list.

10Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and continued use means you accept the update.
